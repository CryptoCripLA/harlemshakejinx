# JINX PROFITS — THE BRAIN IS THE PRODUCT

**One goal:** maximize realized net SOL through intelligent 24/7 trading. Measure trading outcomes; execution success alone is not profit.

## Priority zero: current-position maximum profit

Read the actual current wallet and each current/pending position BEFORE selecting a trade or evaluating a change. Determine spendable SOL, committed SOL, all held tokens, confirmed status, achievable proceeds, active exit ownership, executable liquidity and all costs. Maximize net SOL from the capital JINX ACTUALLY HAS, including tiny wallets. Aim at explosive opportunities and capture the most of their ride; the owner's $2 → $8–$9 example is the targeted multiple, not a fabricated guaranteed fill.

Every path—first ignition, renewed wave, entry, notional sizing, submission, confirmation, monitored hold, peak-sensitive exit, confirmed SOL receipt, and re-entry—is optimized for realized net SOL. Stale or stranded positions are an engineering fault. An actual negative-net test trade is FAILED profitability acceptance: investigate signal, entry, timing, sizing, fee drag, price impact and exit; fix and retest immediately.

## Discover → anticipate → enter
- Audit active market discovery, chatter sources, Solana event timing, pump.fun curve and pool state, liquidity additions, buy/sell pressure, buyer arrival velocity/acceleration, repeat actors, concentration, migration and distribution.
- Identify two intentional entry modes: **first ignition** (before/at early acceleration) and **renewed breakout** (fresh liquidity and sustained acceleration after earlier waves). A token's age alone does not answer whether the *next* wave is profitable.
- Reconstruct exact information available at decision time; compare earliest feasible entry, actual confirmed fill, missed winners, fakeouts and executed versus predicted impact. Do not use future samples to justify past buys.
- Every accepted candidate must be attributable to measured signal evidence and an executable, after-cost round-trip opportunity. Empty FIRE spam is not an edge.

## Ride the wave → peak-aware exit
- Track current executable full-position proceeds, high-water proceeds, marginal price/flow velocity, acceleration, buy-to-sell turnover, exit liquidity/depth, creator and cluster sells, and venue migration in live per-position state.
- Detect fading velocity early; differentiate brief noise from distribution/structural reversal, while retaining room for explosive extensions and second legs. Compare flow-decay, adaptive peak-capture, dynamic trailing, partial versus full exits, and route-specific exit efficacy on matched streams.
- An open position always has a live, durable exit owner; if first sell attempt fails, retain custody and pursue all available current routes with exact reason/receipt.
- **Re-entry:** after actual closure, recognize fresh independent wave structure with a new time-stamped decision; do not replay stale FIREs or block evidence-backed continuation merely due to previous ownership.

## Sizing and economics
- Size for projected net SOL, executable depth, impact and measured edge; quantify chain/protocol/priority/tip costs both directions, ATA/rent when applicable. Do not confuse available-wallet accounting with arbitrary old fee-floor vetoes.
- Reconcile SOL inputs/outputs, filled token quantities, residual inventory, costs, recovered rent and nontrading transfers. Position and wallet P&L are related, not interchangeable.
- Record high-water attainable SOL, actual exit SOL, capture ratio, signal-to-submission latency, fee drag, realized net SOL, misses, and root causes for every closed trade.

## Improvement
Replay historical waves (including failures and nonentries), compare candidate cohorts without lookahead, make one meaningful change, test it immediately, measure net SOL and opportunity capture, repeat. Any negative executed round trip is a **failed profitability acceptance** requiring attribution and renewed engineering—never celebrated as a passing test. Keep actual outcomes visible even while correcting them.
