Kelly Criterion — The Card Counter's Edge
Card counters don't bet the same amount every hand. When the count is high — when the odds are in their favor — they size up. When it's not, they size down. SolBot applies the same math to every trade. A score-60 entry and a score-85 entry are not the same bet. Kelly treats them differently.

▸
Per-user, per-score-bucket Bayesian model
Your trades train your own model. Tony's win rate doesn't affect your position sizes — and yours don't affect anyone else's
▸
Quarter-Kelly default — conservative by design
Full Kelly maximizes long-run growth but creates violent drawdowns. Quarter-Kelly captures the edge with a fraction of the volatility
▸
Self-calibrates from your live trade history
After 20+ closed trades per score bucket, Kelly updates its win-rate and win/loss-ratio estimates from your actual results
▸
Auto-recalibrates when you change entry filters
Change your min score threshold and Kelly resets to conservative sizing — your old priors no longer apply to the new population

▸
Score-adaptive partial exit — not a flat percentage
Higher conviction entries exit less aggressively, letting winners run longer. Lower conviction entries exit faster to protect gains.

Re-entry allowed immediately
No exit cooldown — this is a momentum exit, not a stop-loss. If signals recover, the bot can re-enter the same token.
