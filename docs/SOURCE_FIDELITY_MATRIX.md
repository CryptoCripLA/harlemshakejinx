## C. Execution ownership (owner directive 2026-10-20)

GMGN is the trading platform and JINX is the intelligence (`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/INTEGRATION_MAP.md`). Execution rows (C09 exits, C14, C15, C18) now route through GMGN: swaps, percentage sells, condition orders and order status. JINX keeps its decision and reconciliation records; GMGN's confirmed order reports are the source of truth for GMGN-placed orders.

## D. Phase 1 known deviations

| Item | What and why | Effect |
|---|---|---|
